# Neptune 0.1.0-DEV.6

- Optional Upcoming Releases and Spoilers switch, disabled by default.
- Separate Home and artist release shelves for singles, EPs and albums.
- Independent catalog IDs and no ISRC for previews.
- FLAC, MP3 320 and MP3 128 streaming; embedded artwork from backend.
- Collaborations appear on both artist pages.
- Official search remains unchanged; previews are not advertised through search.
- Official catalog, artist artwork and dynamic CI artifact naming preserved.

Build and device validation pending. Preview filenames use lowercase ASCII letters, digits and hyphens.
